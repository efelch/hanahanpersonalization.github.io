About Us

  • 🪚 Handcrafted woodworking
  • ✨ Precision laser engraving
  • 📍 Locally made in Hanahan, SC

Welcome to Hanahan Personalization! We are Edward and Monica Felch, and we create custom, personalized gifts and decor for important moments. We make everything in our workshop, which includes a wide variety of woodworking tools as well as laser engravers.

What started as a passion for creating meaningful gifts and decorations has grown into our small business where we bring your visions to life. Let us know what you want, and we can make it happen!

To see our Halloween and Christmas designs, head over to Macabre and Mirthworks

We are local first - We believe in supporting local businesses and communities, and we strive to make a positive impact in our community. Proudly crafting custom decor for families in Hanahan, Summerville, North Charleston, and the greater Charleston, SC area.

FAQ

Do you take custom orders?

Yes! Custom orders are most of what we do. Send us a message through our contact form with your idea and timeline, and we'll help bring it to life.

What materials do you work with?

Mostly wood and acrylic, engraved or cut with our laser engravers, plus general woodworking for larger pieces like signs and stands. Ask us if you have a specific material in mind.

What's your typical turnaround time?

It varies by project complexity and our current order queue. We'll give you an estimate when you reach out with your idea.

Local pickup or shipping?

Both! We're based in Hanahan, SC, so local pickup is available, and we also ship within the United States.

Where can I buy your Halloween and Christmas decor?

That side of what we make lives on our sister site, Macabre and Mirthworks.

Serving: Hanahan, North Charleston, Summerville, Goose Creek, and the greater Charleston, SC area.
